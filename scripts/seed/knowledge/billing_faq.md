Invoices are emailed on the day of each charge and are also available from
the account billing page.

A failed payment is retried automatically up to 3 times over 5 days before
a subscription is marked past-due. If a customer reports being charged
despite a failure notice, check get_payment_status before promising
anything - card networks occasionally show a temporary authorization hold
that is not an actual charge and clears within a few days.

Duplicate charges should be verified against payment records before any
refund is promised.

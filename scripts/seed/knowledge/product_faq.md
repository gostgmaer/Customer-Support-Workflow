Our product line includes a free tier, a Pro monthly subscription, and an
annual Pro plan at a discount. Feature comparisons are available on the
pricing page.

The mobile app supports offline mode for previously loaded content only;
syncing resumes automatically once a connection is restored.

File uploads are limited to 50MB per file on the free tier and 500MB per
file on Pro plans. If a customer reports crashes on upload, check whether
the file exceeds their plan's limit before treating it as a bug.

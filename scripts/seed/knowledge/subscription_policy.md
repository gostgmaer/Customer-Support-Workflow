Subscriptions renew automatically at the start of each billing cycle unless
cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals but does not refund the
current billing period; access continues until the end of the period
already paid for.

Customers on an annual plan who cancel within 14 days of the original
purchase are eligible for a prorated refund - route these to the refund
process instead of a plain cancellation.

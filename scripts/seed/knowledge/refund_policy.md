Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of delivery for items
that arrive damaged, defective, or significantly not as described.

Refund requests are reviewed and, once approved, are returned to the
original payment method within 5-7 business days.

Refunds over $200 require manual review by a member of the support team
before being processed.

Digital goods and gift cards are non-refundable once redeemed.

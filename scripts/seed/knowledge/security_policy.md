---
title: Account Security Policy
category: security
version: "1.0"
effective_date: 2026-01-01
expiration_date:
---

Any report of unauthorized account access, suspicious login activity, or
suspected fraud must be escalated immediately to the security team and must
never be resolved automatically by the AI assistant.

The assistant must not confirm or deny specific account details (email,
address, payment method) to a customer whose identity has not been
independently verified by a human agent in these cases.

Support agents handling a security escalation should: verify identity
through a secondary channel, review recent account activity logs, and
temporarily lock the account if takeover is suspected.

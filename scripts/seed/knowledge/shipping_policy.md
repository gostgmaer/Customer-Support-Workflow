Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days within the country of purchase.
Expedited shipping takes 1-2 business days and is available at checkout for
an additional fee.

Once an order ships, customers receive a tracking number by email. Delays
of more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery date should be
reported to support so a replacement or refund can be considered.

Orders can be cancelled free of charge only while their status is "placed".
Orders that have already shipped cannot be cancelled, but may be eligible
for a return once delivered.
